Method verification calculator

Before reporting patient results on a new unmodified FDA-cleared test, CLIA requires you to verify accuracy, precision and reportable range (42 CFR 493.1253). Enter your data and your lab's acceptance criteria.

What CLIA asks for

For an unmodified, FDA-cleared or approved moderate or high complexity test, the lab must show it can match the manufacturer's performance for accuracy, precision and reportable range, and confirm the manufacturer's reference intervals fit its patients (42 CFR 493.1253(b)(1)). Modified tests and lab-developed tests also need analytical sensitivity, specificity and any other relevant characteristics established (493.1253(b)(2)).

Acceptance criteria are set by your laboratory director, often using manufacturer claims or CLIA proficiency testing limits (42 CFR 493 Subpart I). This calculator uses whatever criteria you enter; it does not choose them for you.
