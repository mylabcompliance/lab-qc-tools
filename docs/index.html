Free lab QC tools

Open-source calculators for clinical laboratories. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

Westgard QC Rules Calculator

Paste QC results to check Westgard rules (1-2s, 1-3s, 2-2s, R-4s, 4-1s, 10x) and draw a Levey-Jennings chart. Free, runs in your browser.

Method Verification Calculator

Verify a new test method under CLIA: precision (CV%), accuracy by method comparison (bias, slope, r) and reportable range (recovery). Free.

CLIA Personnel Qualification Checker

Check a lab director, technical consultant, general supervisor or testing person against the current CLIA personnel rules (42 CFR 493 Subpart M).
